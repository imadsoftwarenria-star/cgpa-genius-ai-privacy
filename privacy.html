CGPA Genius AI is developed by IMAD SOFTWARE. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

The application stores academic information such as semesters, subjects, grades, credits, SGPA, CGPA, prediction data and friends comparison data locally on your device.

We do not require account registration and we do not intentionally collect personal information such as passwords, contacts, photos, messages or precise location.

The application uses Google AdMob. Google may collect Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information and usage information according to Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your data remains on your device. We recommend securing your device with a passcode.
